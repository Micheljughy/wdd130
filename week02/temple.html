About the temple

The Abidjan Ivory Coast Temple is the 173rd operating temple of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
It is located in the Cocody district of Abidjan, Ivory Coast.
The temple was announced on April 5, 2015,
and dedicated on June 14, 2019.
